**Recipe: 15 Minute Creamy Pasta Primavera**
**Cuisine:** Italian
**Dietary:** Vegetarian
**Prep/Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Servings:** 2 people

**Description:**
A quick and easy 15-minute dinner packed with fresh vegetables and coated in a creamy, cheesy sauce. This recipe is perfect for a busy weeknight meal.

**Ingredients (Estimated based on recipe type):**
*   Pasta (e.g., penne, fettuccine)
*   Creamy sauce base (e.g., heavy cream, milk, or cream cheese)
*   Cheese (e.g., Parmesan, mozzarella)
*   Mixed fresh vegetables (e.g., broccoli florets, cherry tomatoes, asparagus, peas)
*   Garlic and olive oil
*   Seasonings (salt, pepper, Italian seasoning)

**Instructions:**
1.  Cook the pasta according to package directions until al dente.
2.  While the pasta cooks, sauté the garlic and mixed vegetables in olive oil until tender.
3.  In a separate pan, create the creamy sauce base, stirring in the cheese until melted and smooth.
4.  Drain the pasta and add it directly to the sauce mixture.
5.  Toss everything together until the pasta is evenly coated and heated through.
6.  Season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning before serving.
